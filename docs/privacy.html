Privacy Policy for I'm Just A Cube

Last updated: 22 September 2026

I'm Just A Cube ("the game") is a puzzle game published by Nadav Rutman Moshe, trading as Nadaz Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game does with information, in plain language. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of the game.

The short version. The game itself collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere. Your progress is saved on your own device. Two things do involve other companies, and only when you choose them: watching a rewarded advertisement, and buying something.

1. What the game stores on your device

The game saves the following in your device's local storage, so that it can remember where you were. The game never sends this information to us or to anyone else. If your phone backs itself up (Google's backup on Android, iCloud on iPhone), that backup can include it, so your progress can come back when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. That backup is kept in your own Google or Apple account, and we cannot see it:

  • Your progress: levels completed, moves taken, stars earned, lives and cores on timed levels, levels you skipped and levels you failed.
  • Your settings, including the answer you gave to the one question asked when you first open the game (an age band, or a difficulty choice if you chose not to give an age). We store only which band was chosen, never a date of birth.
  • Your wardrobe: which pieces and colours you own and which you are wearing, the hint pool, and how many advertisements you have watched towards an unlock.
  • Any levels you build yourself in the level editor.

The game makes no network requests of its own. Its typefaces and artwork are inside the app rather than downloaded, so with advertisements never shown and nothing purchased, the game never connects to the internet at all.

2. Advertisements

The game shows rewarded video advertisements only. There are no banners and no advertisements that interrupt play. A video is shown only after you press a button that says it will play one, in exchange for something in the game such as hints or unlocking a level. You can always play without watching any.

Advertisements are provided by Google AdMob. When an advertisement is requested, Google's advertising software collects and shares the following, as described in Google's own disclosure:

3. Children

This game is made for children and adults alike. The first time it opens, it asks one question: your age band. Anyone who does not choose an explicitly adult band - including anyone who selects "under 18" and anyone who prefers not to say - is treated as a child for advertising purposes. For those players:

  • Advertisements are marked as child-directed and as being for users below the age of consent, so Google serves only non-personalised advertisements.
  • No advertising identifier is transmitted.
  • No profile is built for advertising purposes, and no behavioural targeting takes place.
  • No consent form or tracking request is shown to them.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no social features, no photograph or microphone access, and no way to send anything about a player to us. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about this game, please contact us at the address below.

4. Consent in Europe

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland and you are an adult, the first time you press a button to watch an advertisement you will be shown a consent form from Google's certified consent tool, asking whether advertisements may be personalised. You can change that answer at any time from Menu → More → Ad Privacy in the game. Players treated as children are not shown the form and always receive non-personalised advertisements.

5. Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payment is made to them, not to us: we never receive or see your name, your payment card, or your billing address. The store tells the game which items your store account owns, so that the game can unlock them again after a reinstall or on a new device, and the game keeps a copy of that list on your device. Purchases in this game are one-time unlocks; there are no subscriptions.

6. Security, and how long anything is kept

Everything the advertising software sends is encrypted in transit using TLS. We hold no database and no servers, so there is nothing about you for us to keep, lose or hand over. Information saved on your device stays until you delete the game, apart from any copy in your phone's own backup (section 1), which you control from your phone's settings.

7. Your choices

  • Delete everything the game saved: uninstall the game, or use your device's option to clear its data. A copy may also be in your phone's own backup, which you manage from your phone's settings or your Google or Apple account.
  • Reset or delete your advertising identifier: on Android, Settings → Privacy → Ads; on iOS, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking (the game never asks for tracking permission, so it is off unless you turned it on for everything).
  • Change your advertising consent (Europe): Menu → More → Ad Privacy inside the game.
  • Stop seeing advertisements entirely: buy either pass, or simply do not press the buttons that offer a video.
  • Ask us anything, including about information Google holds: write to the address below and we will help as far as we are able. For information held by Google, Google's own privacy tools apply.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version appears at this same address. Material changes will also be noted in the game's store listing.
